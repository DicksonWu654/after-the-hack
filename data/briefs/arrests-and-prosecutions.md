# Legal defence excerpt

"Code is law" and "I only used public functions" defences:

| Case | Defence | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Mango (Eisenberg) | no rules were broken, DAO consented | convicted; counts 1-2 vacated on venue, count 3 acquitted; US appeal pending as of 21 Oct 2026 |
| Platypus brothers | public contract, "ethical hacker" | acquitted at first instance in France (December 2023); appeal status unverified |
| Peraire-Bueno | market speculation, validators acted as designed | hung jury; retrial ordered ready for 2027-06-07; last direct docket check 2026-09-28 |
| Ahmed | pleaded guilty | 3 years |
| Spalletta | full defence submissions were not independently reviewed | convicted on all counts; sentencing reported for 2027-02-16 |
| Medjedovic | publicly argued the code allowed it; no court defence reviewed | no public US custody record found in sources searched through 21 Oct 2026 |
