# Evidence available in this release

Definitions and evidence grades are in `data/canonical_numbers.json`; methods and column definitions are in `README.md` and `data/README.md`. Source links in the data distinguish court records, project statements, firm analysis and reported claims. The data snapshot ends 21 October 2026. Corrections were prepared 23 October 2026.

The full project research briefs and prior negotiation-study incident ledger are unavailable in this release. Citations redirected here state that limit. The prior-study summary tables in `data/negotiation_tables/` reproduce the reported frame totals and comparisons, but readers cannot independently recode the underlying cases from those tables.

The audit corpus, incident-to-audit join inputs and manual precision sample are unavailable in this release. A1's 334 of 4,005 event count and 146 of 1,377 master matches are author calculations with limited public reproducibility. These counts describe incident-to-audit entity matches; audit-failure rates were not measured.

The published master, case records and derived tables are inspectable in `data/`. Scrubbed on-chain verification notes are in `data/briefs/onchain-verification.md`. The legal defence excerpt is in `data/briefs/arrests-and-prosecutions.md`; its primary and secondary source URLs are included with the excerpt.

SlowMist record text is omitted under the source's redistribution terms. The public release retains dates and source links in `data/slowmist_record_links_2026-10-21.csv`. Raw tracker snapshots and private working correction sheets are unavailable; the cited derived tables and current case records state what readers can inspect.
